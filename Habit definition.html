INTRODUCTION TO HABITS

Habits are part of our daily lives. It is defined as a behavior that we repeatedly do every day. In fact, around 40 percent of our behavior are habits and achieving goals or become the person we want to be are primarily caused by our habits (Clear, 2018). As a student, I find it interesting to understand what habit is because having knowledge about it can be used to achieve a student’s potential. Therefore, let us dig in to what is habit, its types, and how does it help us.
