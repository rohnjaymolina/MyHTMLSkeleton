TYPES OF HABITS

GOOD HABITS

Good habits are repeated behaviors that supports our well-being. These behaviors not only bring us good results or achieve something we want, but also, they help us to become better in our lives especially on being someone we want. Example of good habits are eating healthy food, studying consistently, reading books every day, and exercising. Habits like these help us to become something good in our lives (Clear, 2018).

BAD HABITS

Bad habits are negative behaviors that can affect our daily lives. Examples include procrastination, excessive use of social media, staying up late, skipping meals, laziness, and spending too much time playing online games. These habits can waste our time, reduce our productivity, and negatively affect our health. For students, procrastination and poor time management can result in unfinished assignments, low academic performance, and increased stress. Although some bad habits may seem harmless at first, continuously practicing them can create problems that become difficult to overcome (Clear 2018).

Good and bad habits are formed through repeated actions and personal choices. Changing bad habits requires discipline and determination, while developing good habits can help us become more productive and improve our daily lives.
